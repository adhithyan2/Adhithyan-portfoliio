## How to use effectively
- **Send 5–10 messages a day.** Consistency beats bursts.
- **Add their business name.** It doubles the reply rate.
- **Attach your portfolo link** after they show interest: `https://adhithyan-portfoliio.vercel.app/`
- **Follow up once** after 2 days if no reply. Don't spam.
- **Never send a price first** — quote only after they describe their need.
- **Track who replied** in a simple list (name, business, date, status).

## Where to find prospects
- **Google Maps** — search "salon near me", "clinic", "restaurant in [your city]" → find shops with *no website / no booking button*.
- **Instagram** — local business pages that post but have no link/bio website.
- **Facebook** — local groups (city business groups, "digital marketing" local pages).
- **Walk-in** — nearby shops with no online presence; a personal visit works best locally.
